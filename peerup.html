What is

PeerUp?

Learning AppI2025

PeerUp helps working professionals communicate more effectively at the workplace. The platform uses AI to create personalised practice plans, enabling learners to practise spoken English and workplace scenarios with both AI and real peers. With instant feedback and targeted exercises from an AI coach, learners build confidence and improve their communication skills faster.

Problems Identified

  • 17% of users dropped off right after finishing onboarding.
  • PeerUp is built around speaking, but onboarding never made that clear.
  • Onboarding asked a lot of questions but gave users nothing to interact with or learn in return.
  • The home screen offered many options but no clear first step.

Goals to work on

Introduce the apps features and values

Include feature dedicated pricing and purchase screens

Keep the flow short—5 to 7 screens max

Make onboarding feel personal to each user’s goals.
