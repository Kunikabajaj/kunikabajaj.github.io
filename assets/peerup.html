What is

Peer Up?

Learning AppI2025

Peer Up helps working professionals communicate more effectively at the workplace. The platform uses AI to create personalised practice plans, enabling learners to practise spoken English and workplace scenarios with both AI and real peers. With instant feedback and targeted exercises from an AI coach, learners build confidence and improve their communication skills faster.

Problems Identified

  • There was a drop-off of 17% after the onboarding was complete .
  • Although it’s a spoken-first app, that point wasn’t communicated clearly to users
  • The users didn't find the app pleasing, they were not delighted with the experience
  • Its was not clear what to do when a user arrives at the home page

Goals to work on

Introduce the apps features and values

Include feature dedicated pricing and purchase screens

Keep the flow short—5 to 7 screens max

Make onboarding feel personal to each user’s goals.
