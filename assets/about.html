Hi, I'm Kunika.

I'm a hands-on product designer based in Bengaluru, with a little over three years spent at early-stage startups. That usually means being the first, or the only, designer in the room, so I've learned to do a bit of everything: talking to users, mapping flows, building design systems and sitting with engineers until the thing ships.

I'm a curious soul who likes to experiment. I try different styles, keep the ones that work and polish them until they feel right. I'd rather get the grunt work done early so I can spend my time on the details, and on the difference the work makes to the people using it and the business behind it.

The road so far

A few stops worth mentioning.

  1. 2019

    Started design school · Srishti, Bengaluru

    Four years of a Bachelor of Design, learning to look closely, sketch badly, then sketch better.

  2. 2022

    First internship · Front Row

    Audited the website, found the five things holding it back, and redesigned how 500+ students and their instructors talked to each other.

  3. 2023

    My first design system · REFO

    Built components, tokens and documentation from scratch, and tested our forms with more than 50 real people. One confusing field can cost a lot.

  4. 2023

    Watching the numbers move · Yearbook Canvas

    Redesigned the mobile and web experience; monthly visitors went from 700 to 1,600 in three months.

  5. 2024

    Founding designer · PeerUp

    Took a speaking-practice app from zero to launch: the flows, the brand, the onboarding experiments, and an Instagram that grew to 148K followers.

  6. 2026You are here
